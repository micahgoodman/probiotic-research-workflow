## Two child sleep questionnaires admitted from a reader's report (18 October 2026)

A September reader noted that in PMID 41286799 (kefir, children with ADHD) the Sleep
Self-Report found more sleep problems on the kefir while the record showed a null from
actigraphy. The full text (PMC12642372) names two questionnaires: the parent-rated
Children's Sleep Habits Questionnaire (33 items, 33–99) and the child's Sleep Self-Report
(26 items, 26–78), both validated, both with one total, both secondary outcomes. Both pass
the four tests on this record alone, so both joined the sleep list after `sdsc`, the
parent-rated scale before the child self-report, matching the lists' observer-first order.
Recorded: CSHQ no_difference; SSR harm (LS means 39.81 vs 37.40 at week 6, p = 0.02),
both between groups. The analysis is of the 53 completers, the only analysis reported, so
it counts (rule 6); n_randomized was corrected from 53 to 80, the number put into groups.
The trial now appears under "Trials where the choice of score changes the result".

## CESD-R (18 October 2026)

PMID 38658619 used the revised CES-D (CESD-R) and its record already carries the result as
`ces-d`. The revision keeps the 20 items, the 0–60 total and the construct, so it is the
same listed score, and the aliases "CESD-R" and "CES-D-R" were added so the list says what
the record does. This is a judgement call: a reader who wants revisions kept apart should
say so, and the one record would move to `instrument_not_listed`.

## NEXARS (37502424), excluded on 18 October 2026

The paper names four "human" strains each tagged "GH" (Streptococcus thermophilus GH,
Streptococcus salivarius GH NEXARS, Lactobacillus plantarum GH, Pediococcus pentosaceus GH),
provided by NEXARS of Brno, with no product name. "GH" does not tell the four strains
apart and NEXARS is the maker, so under rule 3 the blend is not named. Its two null tier-C
rows (anxiety BAI, depression BDI, 72 people) left with it, and `available.json` lost the
`gh+prebiotics` entry.
